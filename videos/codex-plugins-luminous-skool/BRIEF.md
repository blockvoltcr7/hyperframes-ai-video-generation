## Intent

Create a practical Skool lesson that teaches beginners what Codex plugins are,
what they can contain, how to install and invoke them, and how permissions work.
The tone is confident, visual, and concrete rather than promotional.

## Assets

- Original GPT-generated, text-free luminous teaching illustrations for the plugin bundle, installation path, and permission boundary.
- Two of those illustrations become 15-second local MP4 animation plates through Fal PixVerse C1.

## Customizations

- Apply `$luminous-color-vibe` at Signature intensity: near-black canvas, blue-violet atmosphere, restrained pink highlights, crisp white typography, minimal translucent surfaces.
- Keep all product terminology, instructions, and labels in native HTML/SVG.
- Use longer image-to-video segments than the previous workshop project.

## Notes

- Source facts come from official OpenAI documentation about Codex plugins.
- Stop at a verified HyperFrames Studio preview; rendering requires explicit approval.
- HyperFrames owns narration and sound. Generated video plates must be silent.
