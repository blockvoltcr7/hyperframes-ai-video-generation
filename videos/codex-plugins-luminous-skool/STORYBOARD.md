# Video direction

- Use the Luminous Signature tokens in `frame.md`: near-black navy canvas, cool-white information, electric blue structure, violet transformation, and pink only at terminal actions.
- Pace every frame to the narration. Use long-tail power3 easing for cinematic arrivals, then hold each important read before the next reveal.
- Avoid slideshow behavior: each frame must reveal new visual information across its back half. Avoid looping breathing, screensaver drift, arbitrary zooming, and late-frame exits.
- AI-generated imagery is atmosphere and metaphor, never the source of instructional text. The two generated-video plates are silent, local, poster-backed, and mounted through inert `data-generation-shot-id` slots until reviewed.
- Keep all teaching labels in HTML so they remain crisp, exact, and accessible.
