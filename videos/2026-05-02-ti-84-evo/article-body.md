# TI-84 Evo Graphing Calculator

## Overview

Texas Instruments' latest graphing calculator represents a significant evolution in educational technology. The TI-84 Evo is designed as a "distraction-free learning tool" that maintains focus on mathematics rather than entertainment features.

## Key Features

**Performance Enhancements:**
The device boasts a 3x faster processor compared to previous models, operating at 156 MHz. The graphing display area has expanded by 50%, offering users a 320 x 240 pixel screen with 16-bit color capability.

**Design Improvements:**
Notable upgrades include a USB-C port for modern connectivity, a redesigned icon-based home screen, and a simplified keypad layout that reduces clutter. The calculator features a yellow status bar that provides contextual help without revealing answers directly.

**New Functionality:**
The TI-84 Evo introduces "Points of Interest Trace," which highlights significant features automatically while analyzing functions. A redesigned Lines and Conics app includes equation templates and allows users to trace function intersections.

## Exam Approval & Accessibility

The calculator is approved for use on SAT, PSAT/NMSQT, ACT, AP, and IB exams. It comes in seven color options: white, pink, mint, raspberry, silver, teal, and lavender.

## Value Proposition

Retail purchases include a FREE four-year online calculator license, valued at $80. Schools purchasing teacher packs receive fifty additional four-year licenses per pack of ten units.
